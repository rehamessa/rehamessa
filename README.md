## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Data Analyst & BI Developer** who turns messy operational data into reporting that teams rely on every day. Most of my work is in **Power BI, SQL, Excel, and Power Query**, and I'm currently building up my **Microsoft Fabric** and data engineering skills.

**What I do**

* 📊 **Dashboards & reporting** — build and maintain Power BI dashboards and performance reports for operations and business teams
* 🧩 **Data modeling & DAX** — write measures and calculated logic behind interactive reports that answer specific business questions
* 🔍 **Data cleaning & quality** — find the issues in large, messy datasets and reshape them into structured, reliable reporting data
* ⚙️ **Automation** — replace repetitive manual reporting with Power Query, SQL, and Excel
* 🤝 **Stakeholder work** — gather requirements with operations teams, investigate data issues, and explain what the numbers mean

**KPIs I work with**

`AHT` · `SLA` · `CSAT` · `FCR` · `Adherence` · `Occupancy` · `Utilization` · `Productivity`

**Currently learning** 🌱 Microsoft Fabric · KQL · Apache Spark · modern data engineering workflows
