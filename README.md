## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Data Analyst** with a background in **Business Intelligence**, passionate about transforming raw data into clear, actionable insights. I graduated from the **Information Technology Institute (ITI)** in Egypt and enjoy working across the full data pipeline — from querying and cleaning data to building dashboards that help stakeholders make better decisions.

- 📊 Specialized in **dashboards, reporting, and data storytelling**
- 🔍 Experienced in **exploratory data analysis** and turning messy datasets into structured insights
- 📐 Comfortable with both **statistical analysis** and **visual communication** of data
- 🌱 Continuously expanding my skills in **machine learning** and **predictive analytics**
- 🤝 Enjoy collaborating with stakeholders to translate business questions into data-driven answers
