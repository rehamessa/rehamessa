## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Data Analyst & BI Developer** with hands-on experience in **business intelligence, operational analytics, reporting, and data visualization**. I work with business and operational data to build reliable reporting solutions, automate repetitive processes, and turn complex data into clear insights that support day-to-day decision making.

My experience is mainly focused on **Power BI, SQL, Excel, and Power Query**, with growing experience in **Microsoft Fabric and data engineering technologies**.

* 📊 Build and maintain **Power BI dashboards and performance reports** for operational and business teams
* 🔍 Analyze large and messy datasets, identify data quality issues, and transform them into **structured, reliable reporting data**
* ⚙️ Automate reporting processes using **Power Query, Excel, SQL, and Power BI**
* 📈 Work with **operational KPIs and workforce analytics**, including AHT, SLA, CSAT, FCR, Adherence, Occupancy, Utilization, and Productivity
* 🧩 Develop **DAX measures, calculated logic, and interactive Power BI reports** to answer business questions
* 🏗️ Expanding my skills in **Microsoft Fabric, KQL, Spark, and modern data engineering workflows**
* 🤝 Work closely with stakeholders and operations teams to understand requirements, investigate data issues, and deliver actionable insights
